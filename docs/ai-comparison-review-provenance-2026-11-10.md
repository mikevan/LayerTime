# Review provenance

The interpretation reviews in this overnight experiment are performed by Codex, the same development agent, reading saved outputs against supplied evidence. They are not independent human expert review or user-comprehension studies. Report metadata identifies that reviewer.

This clarification is separate from the original comparison protocol. It does not alter the prespecified endpoint cases, frozen inputs, model conditions or outcome criteria. Post-hoc diagnostic cases and the literal-duration check remain separately labelled.
